## 2. Information We Collect

We only collect minimal data necessary to provide core game features, cloud progress synchronization, authentication, and competitive leaderboards.

### A. Information You Provide Directly
* **Google Account Data (Optional):** When you choose to sign in with Google ("Sign in with Google"), we receive your public profile information (such as your Google User ID, Display Name, Profile Picture URL, and Email Address). This is solely used for authenticating your game account and syncing your progress.
* **In-Game Nickname / Handle:** You may choose a custom username or nickname to identify yourself on the public leaderboards and within the game.
* **Country / Region Selection (Optional):** Used exclusively to display national rankings and country flags on global and regional leaderboards.

### B. Gameplay and Progression Data
* **High Scores and Statistics:** Scores, lines cleared, levels achieved, game modes played (*Classic*, *Survival*, *Time Attack*), and timestamps.
* **In-Game Achievements:** Unlocked trophies and achievement timestamps.
* **Game Settings & Preferences:** Sound effects and music volume levels, haptic feedback toggles, chosen visual themes, and control configurations.

### C. Automatically Collected Technical Information
* **Device & Security Verification (Play Integrity / Firebase App Check):** To prevent cheating, score manipulation, and unauthorized API access, technical device tokens are processed through Google Play Integrity and Firebase App Check.
* **Local Storage Data:** Offline gameplay data, high scores, and preferences are stored locally on your device using standard mobile storage APIs (`localStorage`).

### D. Information We DO NOT Collect
* We **do not** collect precise GPS or real-time location data.
* We **do not** collect biometric, financial, banking, or credit card information.
* We **do not** access your device contacts, microphone, camera, photos, or file system.
* We **do not** track your activity across third-party apps or websites (no cross-app tracking).
* We **do not** sell, rent, or monetize your personal data.

## 4. How We Use Your Information

We process and use collected data strictly for the following purposes:
1. **Core Gameplay & Feature Delivery:** Enable game sessions, record high scores, and unlock achievements.
2. **Leaderboards & Competition:** Display player rankings across global, regional, and friend scoreboards.
3. **Cloud Synchronization:** Allow you to preserve and restore your progress across different devices when signed in with your Google account.
4. **Security & Anti-Cheat:** Maintain fair play by validating game scores and verifying app integrity.
5. **App Improvement & Bug Fixes:** Ensure smooth performance and troubleshoot technical errors.

## 5. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

### Data Security
All data transmitted between the App and our backend systems (Google Cloud / Firebase) is encrypted in transit using **TLS/HTTPS** protocols. Access to cloud databases is protected by strict Firestore security rules and authentication checks.

### Data Retention
* **Account & Profile Data:** Retained as long as your account remains active or until you request its deletion.
* **Leaderboard Entries:** High score entries remain on the leaderboard unless removed due to fair play violations or account deletion requests.
* **Local Device Data:** Stored locally on your device and can be deleted anytime by clearing app data or uninstalling the game.

## 6. User Rights and Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

In compliance with Google Play Data Safety policies, GDPR, and CCPA, you have full control over your personal data:

* **Right to Access & Portability:** You can request a copy of the data associated with your account.
* **Right to Rectification:** You can update your custom username and settings directly within the game.
* **Right to Erasure (Account Deletion):** You have the right to request complete deletion of your account, profile data, and leaderboard records.

### How to Request Account / Data Deletion:
To request the permanent deletion of your user account and all associated Firestore records (including username, Google ID link, and leaderboard submissions), please send an email to:

> 🏷️ **Subject:** `Data Deletion Request - Blocks and Rocks`  
> 📝 **Content:** Please provide your in-game username or the Google account email used to sign in. Requests are processed within 30 days.
