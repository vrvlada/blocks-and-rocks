Blocks and Rocks

Blocks and Rocks is a free puzzle game for web browsers and Android. Players place block shapes on an 8×8 grid, clear full rows and columns to score points, manage cracking stones, granite blocks and ticking bombs, chain combo streaks, and compete on global and country leaderboards. The app uses Google Sign-In only to create your player profile, save your high scores and statistics, and display your name on the leaderboard. It does not access your email content, contacts, files, or any other private data.

Application Purpose & Google Integration

🎮 What is Blocks and Rocks?

Blocks and Rocks is a mobile and web puzzle game designed to challenge spatial awareness, strategic planning, and quick reflexes. Players place randomized block shapes on an 8x8 grid to clear full lines while managing evolving board obstacles like cracking stones, granite blocks, and countdown bombs.

🔐 Why Google Sign-In?

We use Google Sign-In exclusively to offer a seamless, secure player experience:
• Profile Authentication: Sign in securely without needing a separate password.
• Cloud Save & Cross-Device Sync: Automatically backup and sync your high scores, game statistics, unlocked achievements, and custom preferences across all your Android and web devices.

🌍 Leaderboard & Privacy

• Fair Global Leaderboards: Submit high scores to global and national rankings verified against anti-cheat protocols (Firebase App Check).
• Minimal Data: We only request your basic public profile (Google user ID, display name, and email). We never access contacts, private files, payment data, or device sensors.

GAMEPLAY FUNDAMENTALS

How to Play Blocks and Rocks

Easy to pick up, deeply strategic to master. Plan ahead to build massive combo streaks!

1

Place Your Pieces

Every turn gives you 3 unique geometric shapes in your tray. Drag and drop them onto the 8x8 grid. Tap or click any piece before placing to rotate it by 90°.

2

Clear Full Lines

Fill an entire row or column (8 blocks). Completed lines vanish instantly, clearing vital grid space and awarding baseline score points.

3

Chain Massive Combos 🔥

Clearing lines in consecutive turns triggers the Combo Streak multiplier (x2, x3, x4...). Multi-line combos skyrocket your high score!

4

Avoid Game Over

The game ends when none of your 3 tray pieces can fit onto the board. Use your Smasher Hammer or Reroll power-up before you run out of moves!

DYNAMIC CHALLENGES

Hazards & Special Blocks

Unlike ordinary puzzle games, Blocks and Rocks introduces active board hazards that test your tactics.

🪨

Stone Block 2 HP

Spawns periodically across Fibonacci milestones. The first line clear cracks the stone, and a second clear shatters it completely!

🗿

Granite Rock 3 HP

An ultra-tough rock spawning on higher tiers. Requires 3 line clears or a direct Hammer strike to destroy.

💣

Ticking Bomb 3x3 BLAST

Counts down in real time with a visible timer. Clear its line or defuse it with the hammer, or let it detonate to blast a full 3x3 area around it!

⚡

Pulse Surge +250 PTS

A timed energy block. Clear it within the 10-second pulsing window to claim an instant, massive point bonus!

TACTICAL ADVANTAGE

Tactical Power-Ups

Earn extra power-ups by crossing score milestones during each run.

🔨

Hammer (Smasher)

Activate the hammer and tap any single block, granite rock, or bomb on the grid to destroy it instantly. Perfect for freeing up space.

🎲

Reroll Tray

Got an awkward set of pieces? Tap the reroll dice to instantly replace all 3 tray pieces with fresh shapes without losing your turn.

🔄

90° Piece Rotation

Rotate any shape in your tray before dropping it onto the board, giving you maximum flexibility to fit tight spots.
